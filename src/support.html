bsTimer support

bsTimer is a speedcubing timer: an unofficial, modified copy of csTimer with sync between devices, plus an iOS app that adds Bluetooth smart cube and timer support.

Getting started

Open the timer. Hold and release the space bar (or the screen) to time a solve. Settings are under the gear button. Your data stays on the device until you sync or export it.

Smart cubes and Bluetooth timers (iOS app)

iOS Safari has no Bluetooth, so the bsTimer app provides it. In the timer's settings choose the Bluetooth input (a smart cube or a Bluetooth timer) and pick the device from the list the app shows.

The first time, allow Bluetooth when iOS asks. If you said no, turn it on in the iOS Settings app under Apps → bsTimer → Bluetooth.

GAN, MoYu, QiYi, GiiKER and GoCube cubes are supported, as in csTimer. The cube's MAC address is read automatically where the cube allows it; otherwise csTimer asks for it. Turning the cube's faces keeps the screen awake while you scramble.

Syncing between devices

In Export (the upload-arrow button), under the Sync section:

  • "Show my sync code" creates a code and starts syncing. On another device, use "Use a code from another device".
  • Or sign in for sync with Apple ("Sign in with Apple"), Google or WCA.

Sync merges: solves from every device are combined, deletions are respected, and it asks before a sync would delete solves or sessions. "Earlier versions…" restores an older synced copy; "Local backups…" restores this device's copy from before a sync. Settings only sync when "Also sync settings" is on.

iCloud in the iOS app

On the bsTimer website the app syncs to your iCloud automatically, with no sign-in, and keeps the app's settings there, so a reinstall or another iPhone comes back with your solves and settings. The iOS Settings app (Apps → bsTimer → Website) can switch the app to csTimer.net instead. Each website keeps its own solves, and iCloud sync is only for bsTimer.

Moving your data

"Export to file" saves everything (in the app, the share sheet offers Save to Files). "Import from file" in another browser, device or csTimer.net loads it. Importing replaces what's there, and sync asks whether to combine the import with your synced data.

Deleting your data

"Delete synced data…" in the Sync section deletes the copy on the server (and, in the app, the iCloud copy), with its earlier versions and linked sign-ins. This device keeps its own data. To clear a device, clear the website's data in the browser or delete the app. See the privacy policy.
