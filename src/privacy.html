bsTimer privacy policy

This policy covers this website and the bsTimer iOS app when it shows this website. bsTimer is an unofficial, modified copy of csTimer. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Stored on your device

Your solves, sessions and settings are kept in your browser's (or the app's) local storage. They stay there unless you use one of the features below.

iCloud (iOS app)

On the bsTimer website, the app syncs your sessions and solves to your private iCloud database, and keeps its sync code in your iCloud key-value storage. This data is in your Apple account; we cannot read it.

Sync through our server

Sync between devices stores a copy of your sessions and solves on this site's server. It starts when you show or enter a sync code, sign in for sync with Apple, Google or WCA, or use the iOS app on the bsTimer website (which keeps a server copy under a random code saved in your iCloud). "Also sync settings" adds your settings, except device-specific ones. We keep:

  • the synced data, with earlier versions: the newest 20 plus one a day for 120 days;
  • only hashed forms of sync codes, sign-in sessions and the account IDs Apple, Google or WCA give us (not your name or email);
  • with Sign in with Apple, a token Apple issues so that we can revoke the sign-in when you delete your data.

Sign-in sessions end after 180 days without use.

Export to server

csTimer's "Export to server" stores the data you upload under the ID you type, or under your WCA account. Earlier uploads are kept so they can be restored. Anyone who knows a typed ID can read that backup, so use a long, unguessable ID or your WCA account.

Signing in

  • WCA: your WCA name and ID are shown on your device. Our server uses your WCA user number only to work out your storage key, and does not keep it.
  • Google: csTimer's Google backup is saved in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder only this site can read. When you use Google to start sync, our server keeps a hashed form of your Google account ID.
  • Apple: see "Sync through our server" above.

Error reports and logs

When the page hits a script error it sends the error message, file, line and timer version, with no identifiers. To limit abuse, the server counts requests per client using a salted hash of the IP address, kept for at most a day. The web host keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, address requested).

Deleting your data

  • Synced data: in Export, under Sync, choose "Delete synced data". This deletes the server copy and all its versions, the sign-ins linked to it and, in the app, the iCloud copy, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Other devices keep their own local data.
  • Data on your device: clear the website's data in your browser, or delete the app.
  • Export-to-server backups and anything else: contact us (below).
